SMART WEATHER TIPS

Be ready for
any weather.

Simple tips to help you stay comfortable and prepared.

01
☀️

Sunny Weather

Carry sunglasses, drink enough water and avoid staying under direct sunlight for a long time.

02
🌧️

Rainy Weather

Carry an umbrella or raincoat and keep electronic devices protected from water.

03
⛈️

Stormy Weather

Stay indoors when possible and avoid standing near trees or electrical poles during storms.

04
🌫️

Cloudy Weather

Keep a light jacket nearby and check the forecast before planning outdoor activities.

05
💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly, especially on warm days when you may lose more water through sweating.

06
🧥

Dress Smart

Choose clothes according to the temperature and weather conditions of the day.

TODAY'S TIP

Always check the weather before leaving home.
